# Raw working notes, November 2026

These are the raw working notes from the November 2026 research, kept for provenance. They contain superseded and conflicting figures. Do not cite them. The authoritative consolidated version is `reports/LLM progress update late 2026.md`.

- `us_lab_releases.md`: release dates and access status for US labs.
- `chinese_open_weight_releases.md`: Chinese and open-weight releases, prices, the open-closed gap.
- `benchmark_landscape.md`: which benchmarks were introduced, saturated or discredited.
- `benchmark_scores.md`: vendor and independent scores by model.
- `metr_time_horizons.md`: METR time-horizon data and caveats.
- `real_world_impact.md`: adoption, productivity, labor market and education.
- `verification.md`: the later check against primary sources. It overrides the other notes where they differ.
