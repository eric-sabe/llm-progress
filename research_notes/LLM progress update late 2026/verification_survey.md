Retrieved 19 November 2026. Every value below was read from the benchmark owner's own page, paper, repository or data file. The Perplexity survey was used only as a list of claims and starting URLs.

Verdicts: CONFIRMED-PRIMARY (claim matches the owner's source), CORRECTED (owner's source says something different), SECONDARY-ONLY (found only off the owner's site), UNVERIFIABLE (could not be traced).

"Executed by" uses three values: benchmark team, third party, vendor (the model or agent maker ran it and submitted the result).

Access notes:
- openai.com was read through Internet Archive snapshots (URLs given in the rows).
- swe-bench-live.github.io, swe-marathon.org and review-bench.ai render client-side; values were read from the data the page loads (`reports-0605.jsonl`, the JavaScript bundle's embedded trial records, and the static fallback table respectively).
- Epoch leaderboard values come from `https://epoch.ai/data/benchmark_data.zip` (copy fetched 12:12 on 19 Nov 2026).

Tally of the 29 reviews: 15 Flawed, 9 Not enough info, 5 Verified.

Stated reasons for the ARC-AGI-3 "Flawed" verdict (https://epoch.ai/benchmarks/arc-agi-3/review). Epoch says each of the four areas independently meets a Flawed threshold in its rubric.

| Area | What Epoch states |
| :-- | :-- |
| Scoring | The score is action efficiency relative to a human baseline, but models are told only "You are playing a game. Your goal is to win." So it measures "a mixture of capability and propensity towards efficiency". The headline number "is not a percentage, even though it is reported as one" (level score is the squared ratio of human to AI actions). It conflates completion with efficiency. The human baseline is the upper median of first-time players and is harder on harder, more heavily weighted levels; the average human tester would score 48% on the public set. Level scores are capped at 1.15, so efficiency gains beyond about 7% above baseline cannot be distinguished. |
| Elicitation | "The 'Standard' harness substantially under-elicits models." Reasoning is discarded after every action and history is trimmed at 175,000 characters. Every model run at the same effort under both harnesses scored much higher with Provider Adapter, for example GPT-6 Astra (max) 62.7% to 98.6%. |
| Benchmark consistency | Games and toolkit are versioned, but harness, prompt and leaderboard entries are not. The system prompt changed on 20 May 2026 with no version bump and older results were not re-run. |
| Bias in evaluation setup | The verified board claims a $10,000 cap per run, but 19 entries cost more, up to $49,800. Incomplete runs are scored as incorrect without being marked or explained. |
